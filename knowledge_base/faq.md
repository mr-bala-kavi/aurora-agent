# Aurora Electronics — Frequently Asked Questions

## Payments
We accept UPI, all major credit and debit cards, net banking, and EMI on orders
above ₹10,000. Prices on the site are final and include GST. You will receive a
GST invoice by email after every purchase.

## Account
You can update your name, delivery address, and phone number from Account →
Profile. To change your registered email, contact support. We never ask for your
password over chat, email, or phone.

## Order changes
You can cancel or modify an order any time before it is marked "shipped". Once
shipped, please use the returns flow instead. Order status is visible under
Account → Orders.

## Offers
Festive and seasonal offers are applied automatically at checkout. Discount
codes, when available, are entered in the "Apply coupon" box on the cart page.
