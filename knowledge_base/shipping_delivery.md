# Shipping & Delivery

## Timelines
Metro cities: 2–3 business days. Rest of India: 4–7 business days. Delivery
estimates are shown at checkout based on your PIN code.

## Charges
Delivery is free on orders above ₹499. Below that, a flat ₹49 fee applies.
Express next-day delivery, where available, is ₹149.

## Tracking
Once your order ships, you receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can
also ask this assistant to check the status of your orders.

## Serviceability
We deliver across India. A few remote PIN codes may have longer timelines; the
checkout page shows the exact estimate for your address.
