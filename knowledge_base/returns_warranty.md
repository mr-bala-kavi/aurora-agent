# Returns & Warranty Policy

## Returns window
Most products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in
original packaging. Laptops and phones have a 3-day return window due to
activation. Earbuds and other hygiene-sensitive audio items are returnable only
if the seal is intact.

## How to return
Go to Account → Orders → Return, pick a reason, and choose pickup or drop-off.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after
the item passes inspection.

## Warranty
All Aurora-branded products carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering
manufacturing defects. Accidental and liquid damage are not covered. For a
warranty claim, keep your GST invoice and raise a support ticket describing the
issue.

## Damaged or wrong item
If your item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, report it within
48 hours with a photo and we will arrange a free replacement.
