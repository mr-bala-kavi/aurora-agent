# Buying Guide

## Laptops
The Aurora Air 13 (₹89,999) is best for students and travel — ultralight with
18-hour battery. The Aurora Pro 15 (₹1,49,999) is for creators and developers
who need a discrete GPU. On a tight budget, the NovaBook Go (₹42,999) handles
everyday study and browsing.

## Phones
The Aurora Phone X (₹64,999) has a 6.5-inch OLED display and a triple camera —
a strong all-rounder for photography and daily use.

## Audio & Wearables
Aurora Buds (₹8,999) offer active noise cancellation for commuting. The Aurora
Watch 2 (₹18,999) adds GPS and a 7-day battery for fitness tracking.

## Accessories
The 65W GaN Charger (₹2,499) fast-charges laptops and phones. The Aurora
Power Bank 20K (₹3,499) is a 20,000mAh magnetic pack with 20W fast charging.
